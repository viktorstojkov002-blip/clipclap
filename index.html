ClipClap

A personal desktop tool that turns livestream recordings into YouTube Shorts.

ClipClap is a private Windows app made and used by the owner of the ClipClap YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Transcribes a livestream recording and suggests its most interesting moments.
  • Cuts those moments into vertical videos with captions.
  • Shows every clip for review. Nothing is published automatically: the owner watches each clip, edits its title, description and tags, and approves it by hand.
  • Uploads only the approved clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.
ClipClap uses YouTube API Services. It uploads videos to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. By using ClipClap you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
